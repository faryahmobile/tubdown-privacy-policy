1. Who we are

This privacy policy explains how TubDown (Android package com.faryahmobile.tubdown), a downhill racing game published on Google Play by Faryah Mobile ("we", "us"), handles your information. Faryah Mobile is the data controller for the data described here.

Privacy contact: faryahmobile@gmail.com · Website: faryahmobile.github.io

2. Summary

  • You can play without creating an account. We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or microphone.
  • Your progress is stored on your device. If you use Google Play Games, a copy is kept in your Google Play Games cloud save and your scores can appear on Play Games leaderboards.
  • We use Google services to run the game: Google Play Billing (purchases), Google Play Games Services (sign-in, leaderboards, cloud save), Firebase Analytics and Remote Config (usage statistics and settings), and Google AdMob with its mediation partners (ads).
  • Ads may use your device's advertising ID. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first; elsewhere you can limit or reset ad tracking in your device settings.
  • We do not sell your personal data for money.

3. Information stored on your device

To run the game, TubDown stores on your device: game progress (stars, fame, levels, records, unlocked parks and chutes), items you own and your purchase records, settings (language, sound, frame rate, notifications), the driver name and race number you choose (shown on your tub and in videos you share), daily/season progress, and a local run log (score, time). This data stays on your device unless you use Play Games cloud save (section 4) or share something yourself (section 6). It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

5. Advertising

  • TubDown shows occasional interstitial ads when you leave the results screen, and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch for a reward. Ads are never shown in the middle of a run.
  • Our ad providers and their partners may use identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, and similar technologies, to show, measure and (where allowed) personalize ads.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, a Google consent form asks for your choices. You can change them any time in Settings → AD PREFERENCES. Our analytics follow the same choice.
  • Other regions (including US states with privacy laws): you can opt out of personalized ads, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, wording varies by device). Where a privacy-choices message is required by law, it is shown in the game and the same Settings entry lets you change it.
  • We limit ad content to a teen-appropriate rating. The "Zero Ads" purchase removes interstitial ads permanently.

6. Things you choose to share

  • Best-moment videos and result cards are created on your device. They leave your device only when you tap Share and pick an app; that app's own policy then applies. A video can show your driver name.
  • Challenge links contain a short code (park, chute, score) and the driver name you chose. They open a page on faryahmobile.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep technical logs (such as IP address) under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Notifications and permissions

Reminders (for example "your daily gift is ready") are scheduled on your device only after you allow notifications; there is no push server. At most one per day; you can turn them off in Settings. TubDown does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or file access. Permissions it uses: internet and network state, notifications (asked on Android 13+), Google Play Billing, advertising ID, and "run at startup" only to restore scheduled reminders after a reboot.

8. Legal bases (EEA/UK) and KVKK

We process data to provide the game you asked for (progress, purchases, cloud save), on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the game working, secure and improving (non-personalized analytics, fraud prevention), and on your consent where the law requires it (personalized ads and related analytics storage in the EEA, UK and Switzerland). For users in Türkiye, processing follows Law No. 6698 (KVKK); data may be transferred abroad to the service providers above, whose servers are located outside Türkiye.

9. Retention

  • Device data: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Play Games cloud save and leaderboard scores: until you delete them in your Google account / Google Play Games settings.
  • Firebase Analytics user-level data: deleted automatically after 14 months. Aggregated reports without user identifiers may be kept longer.
  • Ad data: kept by Google and mediation partners according to their own policies.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer, then at most 1 year.

10. Security

Data sent by the game goes over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) to Google and partner services. Purchases are checked with Google Play's signature before items are delivered. We do not run our own servers that store your personal data.

11. Your choices, rights and deletion

  • Delete game data on your device: Android Settings → Apps → TubDown → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the game. Purchases can be restored later with Settings → RESTORE PURCHASES.
  • Delete Play Games data: through Google Play Games settings or your Google Account (myaccount.google.com).
  • Ads: change consent in Settings → AD PREFERENCES (where shown) and reset/delete your advertising ID in device settings.
  • Analytics and other requests: email us at faryahmobile@gmail.com. Clearing the app's data also resets the analytics ID, so new data is no longer linked to the old one.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, Türkiye or US states such as California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. We answer within 30 days.

12. Children

TubDown is not directed to children under 13 and its Google Play target audience is 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes are also announced in the game.

5. Reklamlar

  • TubDown, sonuç ekranından çıkarken ara sıra geçiş reklamı ve ödül için kendi seçiminizle izlediğiniz isteğe bağlı ödüllü videolar gösterir. İniş sırasında asla reklam gösterilmez.
  • Reklam sağlayıcılarımız ve ortakları, reklamları göstermek, ölçmek ve izin verilen yerlerde kişiselleştirmek için Android reklam kimliği gibi tanımlayıcıları ve benzeri teknolojileri kullanabilir.
  • AB, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre: herhangi bir reklam istenmeden önce Google'ın onay formu tercihlerinizi sorar. Tercihlerinizi istediğiniz zaman Ayarlar → REKLAM TERCİHLERİ'nden değiştirebilirsiniz. Analitik de aynı tercihe uyar.
  • Diğer bölgeler (gizlilik yasası olan ABD eyaletleri dahil): kişiselleştirilmiş reklamları kapatabilir, reklam kimliğinizi cihaz ayarlarından sıfırlayabilir veya silebilirsiniz (Ayarlar → Gizlilik → Reklamlar; adı cihaza göre değişir). Yasanın gerektirdiği yerlerde oyunda bir gizlilik tercihleri mesajı gösterilir ve aynı Ayarlar girişinden değiştirilebilir.
  • Reklam içeriğini gençlere uygun bir sınıfla sınırlarız. "Sıfır Reklam" satın alması geçiş reklamlarını kalıcı olarak kaldırır.
